Operations & Staging

Every Saturday, our teams deploy from Cooper Medical School directly into the Camden community. Our primary Saturday Outreach operations stage out of 303 Atlantic Avenue, providing hygiene kits, warm clothing, and direct wellness checks to those living on the streets.

Specialized Screening

The BP Project

The BP Project is dedicated to taking the blood pressure of unhoused individuals across Camden, actively screening them for hypertension.

Cooper-Rowan Clinic Patient Recruitment

When elevated blood pressure is detected, our immediate goal is to provide a direct referral to the student-run Cooper-Rowan Clinic. This targeted effort bridges the gap between on-the-street vitals checks and long-term, continuous primary care for the uninsured.

Recreation & Wellness

The Camden Fishing Club

Healthcare extends beyond clinical vitals. The Camden Fishing Club is a specialized community-building initiative providing unhoused Camden residents with a structured outlet for recreation, dignity, and mental decompression.

Through secured grant funding, this program supplies unhoused individuals with:

  • Quality fishing rods and tackle kits
  • Prepaid New Jersey fishing licenses to prevent regulatory fines
  • Custom-mapped local waterway guides

By shifting the dynamic from "patient and provider" to peers sharing time along the Cooper River banks, medical students and residents build profound trust that translates directly into better health outcomes and clinic follow-ups.
