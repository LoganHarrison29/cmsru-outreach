Shift Protocols & Checklists

Field Gear & Dress Code

  • ✓ Wear closed-toe, comfortable walking shoes.
  • ✓ Display your Cooper student ID visibly.
  • ✓ Dress in layers appropriate for prolonged outdoor weather.
  • ✓ Avoid wearing flashy jewelry or bringing large valuables.

Pre-Flight Briefing Preview

You will receive this automated brief 24 hours prior to your shift:

"Parking is available on Atlantic Ave. Arrive 10 mins early for inventory count. If driving, ensure your trunk is empty to load hygiene kits. Review the street de-escalation guide before arrival."

Mobile Unit Kit Checklist

  • Automated & Manual BP Cuffs (Adult/L/XL)
  • Stethoscopes & Alcohol Prep Pads
  • Cooper-Rowan Clinic Referral Slips
  • Bilingual Patient Number Cards

Street Etiquette Orientation

Approach all community members at eye level. Always ask for consent before handing items or taking vitals. Use trauma-informed language and respect personal boundaries at staging zones.

Shift Leadership Structure

Each Saturday operates with 1 Driver (transports kits), 1 Shift Leader (manages flow and vitals charting), and 4 Volunteers. Escalate clinical anomalies directly to the attending Faculty Preceptor.

Post-Shift Debrief

At 12:15 PM, teams gather to discuss: What went well? Were there any challenging patient encounters? What supply shortages did we notice for next week?
